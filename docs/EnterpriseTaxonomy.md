# Enterprise Taxonomy

## Purpose

The Enterprise Taxonomy defines the controlled vocabulary used across the QualityFirst Technologies SharePoint Online platform.

The taxonomy provides a standardized classification framework that supports:

- Information Architecture
- Content Classification
- Enterprise Search
- Records Management
- Retention Management
- Power Automate Integration
- Governance
- Copilot Readiness

The taxonomy serves as a foundational component of the organization's Information Governance and Information Architecture strategy.

---

# Taxonomy Design Principles

The Enterprise Taxonomy shall be designed and maintained according to the following principles:

- Business-driven terminology
- Reusable controlled vocabulary
- Consistent naming standards
- Minimized duplication of terms
- Enterprise-wide applicability
- Support for Information Architecture
- Support for Enterprise Search
- Support for Records Management
- Support for Automation
- Support for future AI and Copilot scenarios

# Taxonomy Governance

## Purpose

Taxonomy governance ensures that the Enterprise Taxonomy remains accurate, consistent, scalable, and aligned with business requirements.

A governed taxonomy supports records management, content classification, enterprise search, metadata consistency, and future AI initiatives.

## Roles and Responsibilities

### Taxonomy Administrator

Responsible for:

- Managing the Enterprise Term Store
- Creating and maintaining Term Groups
- Creating and maintaining Term Sets
- Approving taxonomy changes
- Maintaining naming standards
- Coordinating taxonomy reviews

### Records Manager

Responsible for:

- Record classification definitions
- Lifecycle status definitions
- Retention category management
- Records management alignment
- Taxonomy compliance monitoring

### Business Owners

Responsible for:

- Maintaining business terminology
- Identifying new classification requirements
- Requesting taxonomy updates
- Validating business-specific terms

### Microsoft 365 Administrator

Responsible for:

- SharePoint Term Store administration
- Metadata service configuration
- Technical governance controls
- Platform maintenance and support

# Taxonomy Maintenance

The Enterprise Taxonomy shall be reviewed periodically to ensure continued alignment with organizational requirements.

## Review Frequency

- Quarterly review of Term Sets
- Annual review of taxonomy structure
- Ad-hoc reviews based on business requirements

## Change Management Process

```text
Business Requirement
        ↓
Taxonomy Change Request
        ↓
Review by Business Owner
        ↓
Review by Records Manager
        ↓
Approval by Taxonomy Administrator
        ↓
Implementation
        ↓
Communication to Stakeholders
```
